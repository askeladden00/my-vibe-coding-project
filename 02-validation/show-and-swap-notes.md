# Show & Swap Notes

> Module 2 · Validation. Put the prototype in front of a peer. Their reaction is the data.

## What I showed

https://claude.ai/artifact/MpkkYNsHSrDQ34fK3agyQU; showed a guided activation experience for new B2B SaaS accounts

## What my partner understood (in their words)

You’re testing whether a guided onboarding flow helps new users understand the product and get to value faster

## Where they got confused / pushed back

It wasn’t clear that completing the onboarding actually meant I got value. I wanted to do something and see a real result

## What I'll change as a result

Make the “aha” moment more obvious by having the user take a meaningful action and see the result, rather than treating completion of the flow as success
